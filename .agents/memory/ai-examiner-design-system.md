name: AI Examiner visual system
description: The durable visual-language decision for keeping every AI Examiner screen connected.
---

Use the shared AI Examiner brand tokens as the source of truth for future UI work across the public site, authenticated role workspaces, and evaluation desk. New screens may have different information layouts for admins, teachers, and students, but they should inherit the same deep navy, cobalt blue, warm yellow, warm paper, border, and typography language rather than introducing a separate palette.

**Why:** Separate page-specific palettes made the authenticated experience feel like a different product even when the layout and content were related.

**How to apply:** Update the shared brand token block first when the product palette changes; map page-local tokens back to it and avoid adding new standalone accent colors unless a state requires one for accessibility.
